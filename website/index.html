A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does OverAI give me free access to paid AI models?

OverAI itself is completely free. AI websites keep their own account requirements, subscriptions, and usage limits. OverAI doesn’t unlock paid models.

Do I need an API key?

No AI API key is needed. Use a provider’s free website plan or your existing paid plan. Sign in to each AI website using your existing account, just as you would in a browser.

Will I have to sign in every time?

OverAI keeps website sessions in a persistent WebKit data store on your Mac. Providers can expire sessions or request verification. Safari and Chrome logins aren’t imported.

Will it appear in my screen share?

OverAI includes capture protection for its own app windows on supported capture paths. Compatibility varies with macOS and meeting or recording software. Always test your setup; it cannot guarantee invisibility or hide system dialogs.

Does it work offline or run a local AI model?

The included AI websites need an internet connection. OverAI doesn’t bundle a local model or automatically install Ollama, Docker, or Open WebUI.

What about memory use?

The app loads websites as you visit them and limits background activity. The AI websites still use memory, and visited pages are retained to preserve their state. Usage varies with your websites and conversations.

Which Macs does it support?

OverAI supports Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. The current free preview is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. macOS Gatekeeper may block it; read the release notes before installing.
